# Prerequisites and dependency map

**Who this course is for.** A student comfortable with high-school algebra (vectors, sums, inequalities) who has never seen a proof assistant and never trained a neural network. Nothing else is assumed: every mathematical tool is either reviewed below, built in Lean inside the course, or declared as a named hypothesis when real analysis is out of scope.

**Indicative load.** 25 chapters ≈ 25 weeks at 5–7 h/week (reading + Lean code + graded exercises). The MNIST chapters add one download step (`scripts/get-mnist.sh`).

## The six preparation blocks

Each block lists what you must be able to *do* before the chapters that first demand it, and where the course covers it.

1. **Linear algebra** — vectors, matrices, dot products, norms, dimensions, index arithmetic on bounded ranges. First demanded: Ch01–Ch03, Ch09. Lean: `Vec R n`, `Mat R m n`, `Fin` indices — built in Ch01–Ch03.
2. **Analysis** — derivative, partial derivative, chain rule, gradient as the vector of partials. First demanded informally: Ch03–Ch04. The course does **not** construct the real derivative as a limit (no Mathlib); the differentiation table of `Expr` is verified by reverse-mode tests, and its link to the classical derivative is declared as a boundary (see Ch04 and the proof contract).
3. **Probability** — finite distributions, expectation, variance, independence. First demanded: Ch05 (loss), Ch10 (ELBO), Ch11 (minimax), Ch13 (returns). Lean: `Dist n` (strictly positive weights on `Rat`) in Ch16 — **read Ch16 “Expectation” before Ch10**.
4. **Information** — logarithm laws, entropy, cross-entropy, KL divergence, Gibbs' inequality. First demanded: Ch05 (softmax loss), Ch10 (ELBO), Ch15/Ch17 (contrastive losses). Lean: `LawfulLog` in Ch16 — the log is an abstract operation with named laws; the real logarithm is not constructed.
5. **Optimization** — convexity at the level of quadratic functions, learning rates, stability conditions, gradient descent as a dynamical system. First demanded: Ch05, Ch20. Lean: the Ch05 kit (`sq_le_one`, `powN_le_one`), Ch20 (`recipe_iter_sq_le`).
6. **Reinforcement learning** — state, action, reward, return, discount, Bellman equation. First demanded: Ch13. Lean: one-state Bellman and TD in Ch13; the finite-MDP Bellman operator (fixed policy) with sup-norm contraction, unique fixed point and geometric value-iteration rate is proved in `Ch13/Bellman.lean`; the optimal (max over actions) operator and stochastic Q-learning convergence remain out of scope.

## Two tracks, one course

- **Track “understand deep learning”** — read every chapter, run every demo (`lake exe chNN`), do the fundamental and application exercises; treat proofs as readable specifications.
- **Track “formalize in Lean”** — same reading, plus the deepening exercises and the `Solutions.lean` files; the goal is to write and prove your own lemmas on the course's structures.

Both tracks share every chapter: Lean is the medium, deep learning is the subject. Evaluation: exercises (fundamental + application) certify the track-1 goals; completed proofs of deepening exercises certify track-2 goals; the three [integrative projects](projects) certify the combined goal.
